With chiplets at the forefront of semiconductor integration, the factory is ready to support its global customers and partners. Singapore, July 2023 – Silicon Box, a cutting-edge semiconductor startup, today unveiled its SGD 2 billion advanced semiconductor packaging fab aimed at revolutionising the chip manufacturing sector, developing local capabilities, and boosting Singapore’s position as a global hub for semiconductor manufacturing. Silicon Box will hire and train thousands of high-level employees, with the support of the Singapore Economic Development Board (EDB).
